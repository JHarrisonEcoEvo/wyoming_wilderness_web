I have driven past this WSA, but not walked it. It is easy to get here from South Pass, Wyoming. Just take the Oregon Buttes road and start walking when you feel like it. Low-clearance cars can get here, when the road is dry.

- [BLM — Oregon Buttes WSA](https://www.blm.gov/programs/national-conservation-lands/wyoming/oregon-buttes-wsa) — official BLM page; the buttes rise to 8,612 ft out of the Red Desert's northwestern edge, two-track roads ring the boundary
